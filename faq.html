Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your first breath-hold with us.

Reaching Marsa Alam Freedivers is easy. You can fly into either Marsa Alam International Airport or Hurghada International Airport. Marsa Alam Airport is the closest option, while Hurghada often offers a wider selection of flights and can be a great alternative.

No matter how you arrive, we've got you covered — we can arrange transportation from Marsa Alam International Airport, Hurghada International Airport, any hotel or resort in the Marsa Alam area, and nearby villages, towns, and cities.

We believe Marsa Alam is a freedivers' paradise — temperate conditions year-round, with a minimum water temperature in winter months (Dec–Feb) of 22°C and a maximum in summer months of 30°C. There's a variety of dive sites, some open and some completely protected, so we can select the right site for the day depending on conditions.

We understand that international news coverage of events in the Middle East may raise concerns for some travellers. We'd like to reassure all our guests that life in Marsa Alam continues as normal, and the town remains one of the Red Sea's safest and most peaceful destinations.

Known worldwide for its crystal-clear waters, untouched coral reefs, and incredible marine life, Marsa Alam is a paradise for freedivers, divers, and ocean lovers seeking a relaxed and unforgettable experience. If this is your first visit to Egypt or Marsa Alam, it's completely natural to have questions — rest assured that Marsa Alam is far from areas of unrest and welcomes visitors from around the world throughout the year.

Marsa Alam is well equipped to support water sports activities, with access to medical facilities, emergency services, and healthcare professionals experienced in treating diving-related injuries. In the unlikely event of a diving emergency, assistance can be arranged quickly, and hyperbaric chamber facilities are accessible when required. Your safety is always our highest priority.
